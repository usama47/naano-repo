Record a **product walkthrough of the clone**, not a code tour. Aim for **8–12 minutes**. Show the live UI, then briefly compare it to [naano.com](https://naano.com/).

## Before you hit record

1. Start the app (`pnpm dev` in `naano-web`) and open `http://localhost:3000` in Chrome.
2. Close Slack, email, extra tabs. Hide bookmarks. Zoom to **100%**. Window around **1440×900**.
3. Dismiss the cookie bar once so it does not sit on every shot.
4. Open these tabs in order so you can click through without typing:
   - `/` Home  
   - `/creators`  
   - `/agencies`  
   - `/login` then `/register`  
   - `/blog` (open one article)  
   - `/free-tools` (open one calculator and actually use it)  
   - `/case-studies/blogseo`  
   - `/book`  
   - `/pricing` `/about` `/help`  
   - `/dashboard` (if it loads)
5. Install [Loom](https://www.loom.com/) (Chrome extension is easiest). Choose **Screen + camera** in the corner, **mic on**, camera small.
6. Do one silent dry run of the click path so you know what is slow or broken. Skip anything that 404s.

P00–P10 are the polished slice (home through book). Do not linger on unfinished routes (reports, `/for/*`, leftover `/marketplace`).
